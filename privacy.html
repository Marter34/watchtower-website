Privacy Policy

Information about privacy when using Watchtower and its website.

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Watchtower is a Discord moderation bot and associated website designed to help communities manage their servers. This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information that may be processed when you interact with the bot or website.

The actual information processed depends on which features are enabled, how the bot is configured, and which services are connected. This policy should be reviewed against the bot's actual implementation before publication.

2. Information that may be processed

Discord information

Depending on the commands and features in use, Watchtower may process Discord identifiers and related information, such as:

  • Server IDs and channel IDs.
  • User IDs and usernames needed to identify members.
  • Role and permission information needed for moderation.
  • Moderation actions, reasons, and timestamps.
  • Message content when a feature requires it and Discord makes it available.

Website and technical information

When you access the website or its backend, the hosting provider or connected infrastructure may process technical information such as request details, timestamps, error reports, and security logs.

Support information

If you contact the Watchtower team, information you choose to provide may be processed to respond to your request. Avoid sending passwords, bot tokens, or other secret credentials.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Execute requested moderation commands.
  • Maintain server settings and feature state.
  • Record moderation events or security-related activity when logging is implemented.
  • Investigate errors, abuse, and service reliability issues.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect the service and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information should only be processed for purposes relevant to the operation, security, and support of the service.

4. Storage and retention

Information may be stored in databases or logs used by Watchtower and its hosting providers, depending on the features implemented and enabled.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the operational needs of the service, and applicable legal requirements. Information should not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for its stated purpose.

The exact database fields, retention periods, and deletion processes must be confirmed against the current deployment. This policy does not guarantee that every category of information is stored or that a particular deletion tool is available.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Watchtower may rely on third-party services to operate, including:

  • Discord: the platform where the bot operates.
  • Hosting providers: infrastructure that runs the website or bot.
  • Database providers: storage for information used by supported features.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users. The operators of Watchtower should not sell personal information or share it for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

6. Message content

Some moderation systems may need access to message content to identify spam, prohibited material, or other rule violations. Actual access depends on Discord permissions, enabled intents, and the bot's implemented features.

Do not assume that Watchtower reads, stores, or deletes every message. The deployed bot's code and configuration determine what it can access and what it does with that information.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No internet service, database, or software system can guarantee absolute security. Server administrators should restrict staff permissions and keep their Discord accounts secure.

8. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law and the information involved, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

To make a privacy request, contact the Watchtower team through its official support channels. Include enough information to understand the request, but do not share passwords, authentication tokens, or unnecessary personal details.

Some records may need to be retained for security, operational, or legal reasons. Any request will be handled subject to applicable law and the actual capabilities of the service.

9. Children's privacy

Watchtower is designed for use within Discord communities, not as a service specifically directed at children. Users must meet the age requirements applicable to their use of Discord and comply with relevant laws.

If you believe personal information has been provided or processed inappropriately, contact the Watchtower team through its official support channels.

10. International processing

Depending on the location of hosting and database providers, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Applicable safeguards and legal requirements depend on the locations and services involved.

11. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Watchtower's features, infrastructure, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date when appropriate.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Watchtower team through the official support channels associated with the bot.

Before publishing: Confirm that this policy accurately describes the deployed bot, its database, logs, hosting providers, retention practices, and contact method. This is a template, not legal advice.
